19.2. Using the Drug Search

Pressing Drug Search will bring up a list of the drugs in the system, and their pack sizes. If you enter a chapter of a drug name, the system will shorten the list by hiding all those drugs whose names do not contain the characters you have entered. You can also scroll through the list of drugs using the arrows on the right hand side. Note that the search is not case sensitive, ie. AB, ab and Ab are the same.

When you have found the drug you are looking for, click on the entry in the list. This will close the Drug Search screen and bring up the relevant information about the drug you selected. Clicking Cancel from the Drug Search screen will close the screen but not bring up information about any drug.
