2.2. The Cell-Life Helpdesk

The Cell-Life Helpdesk is available between 9am and 5pm, Monday to Friday. If you have a problem with the system that you cannot solve using the documentation provided, please feel free to call us. iDART is a chaptericipatory design project. This means that all comments from our users are very important, and are often used to guide the design of the system. If you call the helpdesk with a specific problem, try to call while sitting in front of your computer with iDART loaded, as we will usually want to talk you through the task you are performing. If this isn't possible, have a pen and paper ready. The helpdesk can be reached by:

  • Calling +21 21 469 1111, Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm
  • Emailing iDART@Cell-Life.org.za
