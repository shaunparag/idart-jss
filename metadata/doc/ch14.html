Chapter 14. Drug Groups

Drug groups serve two purposes:

  • When adding drugs to a new prescription for a patient, you can select a drug group and all the drugs in the drug group will be added to the prescription, speeding the prescription capture process.
  • Each drug group is associated with a regimen. This means that iDART can count how many patients are on each regimen by comparing the  drugs on the patients' prescriptions with the drugs in the drug group. For this reason, it's important that the drug groups are captured accurately.

Examples of drug groups that would be associated with regimen 1A could be

  • Stavudine 30mg BID, Lamivudine 150mg BID, Efavirenz 600mg QD
  • Stavudine 40mg BID, Lamivudine 150mg BID, Efavirenz 600mg QD
