4.1. Pre-packaging ARVs for patients

For quick dispensing, iDART allows the pharmacist to pre-package a patient's ARVs. The patient may then collect their pre-packaged ARVs at the pharmacy or at a remote clinic. The date on which the ARVs were pre-packaged is recorded by iDART to assist with the tracking of the packages.

4.1.1. Collection at the pharmacy

If the patient collects the packaged ARVs at the pharmacy, the date on which the package is collected as well as the date on which the package leaves the pharmacy are recorded as the current day.

4.1.2. Collection at the local clinic

Before a patient can collect the packaged ARVs at the local clinic, the packaged ARVs need to be transported from the pharmacy to the remote/local clinic. The date on which the packaged ARVs leave the pharmacy is recorded. The packaged drugs are then transported to the clinic. The clinic eventually receives the packaged ARVs and this date is recorded. Finally, when the patient arrives to collect his/her packaged ARVs, the date for collection is recorded.
