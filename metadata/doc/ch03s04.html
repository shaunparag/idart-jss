3.4. Down Referral or Decanting to remote clinics

One of the great features of iDART is that it also allows for the decanting of packages to remote clinics and dispensaries that do not hold stock of ARVs. iDART allows a central ART site or pharmacy to prepare packages for a patients belonging to remote clinics and will trace the entire process until the patient collects their drugs. Feedback is then provided via the network to the central pharmacy to signal that the package was collected and the pharmacist is then allowed to package drugs for the patient in the next month. For more information on the decanting system please see the Cell-Life website. If you have any further queries on iDART please visit our website at www.Cell-Life.org or call 021 469 1111.
